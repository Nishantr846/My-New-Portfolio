About Me

Hi, I'm Nishant Kumar, a Computer Science graduate and Data Engineer at Deloitte India in the Technology & Transformation practice. I specialize in Data Engineering, Data Analytics, and Generative AI, building scalable data pipelines and intelligent applications that transform raw data into actionable business insights.

I am a Databricks Certified Data Engineer Associate with hands-on experience in Python, SQL, PySpark, Databricks, Delta Lake, and Medallion Architecture. My work focuses on developing reliable ETL/ELT pipelines, implementing incremental data ingestion, ensuring data quality, and building analytics-ready datasets using modern data engineering technologies.

At Deloitte India, I am strengthening my expertise in enterprise data and analytics solutions, working with technologies and concepts such as Databricks, PySpark, SQL, Power BI, Advanced Excel, and data-driven reporting to support business insights and informed decision-making.

One of my key projects is FinLake, an end-to-end banking data platform built using Databricks, PySpark, Delta Lake, and Unity Catalog. It implements a metadata-driven Medallion Architecture with Bronze, Silver, and Gold layers, incremental ingestion, data quality checks, quarantine handling, CDC, and SCD Type 2 processing to simulate a production-oriented data engineering workflow.

Alongside data engineering, I have practical experience in Generative AI and Large Language Models. During my internship at Kokku Proptech, I fine-tuned TinyLlama-1.1B using LoRA, developed a domain-specific chatbot using Flask, Hugging Face, and vLLM, and implemented an LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation framework to assess response quality.

I also built ResuMentor, an AI-powered career assistant that analyzes resumes, recommends relevant skills, generates personalized cover letters, and supports interview preparation using LangChain and the Google Gemini API.

Another project, GreenGLOW, is a plant disease detection and care platform powered by a CNN model trained on more than 87,000 images, achieving approximately 97% classification accuracy and providing intelligent plant care recommendations. I also developed Sortify, an interactive web application that visualizes sorting algorithms through real-time animations.

My technical toolkit includes Python, SQL, PySpark, Databricks, Apache Spark, Delta Lake, Unity Catalog, ETL/ELT, Power BI, Azure Data Factory, TensorFlow, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, LangChain, LangGraph, RAG, and AI Agents. I am particularly interested in modern data platforms, lakehouse architecture, intelligent automation, and the integration of AI with enterprise data systems.

I enjoy solving complex technical problems, continuously learning emerging technologies, and translating innovative ideas into practical, scalable solutions. My goal is to bridge the gap between data engineering, analytics, and AI to create meaningful business impact.

Thanks for visiting my portfolio. I'm always open to connecting with like-minded professionals, exploring new technologies, and collaborating on impactful data and AI initiatives.
